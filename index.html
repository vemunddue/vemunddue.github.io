Interface studies · 2026

Ordinary components, slightly wrong conditions.

Six standard interface elements, each doing its job under a small change in physics. They keep their function, their labels and their keyboard support. Not all of them keep their shape.

Anything that breaks can be repaired with the glue in the corner.

Notes

  1. Every break is reversible. The glue is an undo with a physical form: pick it up and apply it where the damage is.
  2. Semantics stay intact. Assistive technology meets an ordinary listbox, switch, field and checkbox, whatever the pixels are doing.
  3. The keyboard works throughout. Activating the glue from the keyboard repairs everything at once.
  4. Reduced motion is respected. Things still break. They just don't perform it.
