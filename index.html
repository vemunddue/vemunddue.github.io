Notes

  1. Everything is reversible. When something breaks, a reset appears next to it and puts every piece back where it came from.
  2. Semantics stay intact. Assistive technology meets an ordinary listbox, switch, field and checkbox, whatever the pixels are doing.
  3. The 3D is real DOM. No canvas, no WebGL. The text field you turn around is a real input, with focus, selection and a label.
  4. Reduced motion is respected. Things still break. They just don't perform it.
