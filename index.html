Liner notes

Every part of the machine is an ordinary, labelled interface element. The sound is synthesised live in your browser. Double-click any knob to put it back.

Side A

  1. Speaker grilles A few hundred checkboxes, lit by the beat.
  2. Woofers Two buttons. Hit them. Kick left, snare right.
  3. Tuner A range input and a knob, 88 to 108. Four stations, static in between.
  4. Filter, echo, reverb, drive Sliders you turn. Drag up and down, or use the arrow keys.
  5. Equalizer Ten range inputs standing on end.

Side B

  1. Transport Five buttons that latch, like they used to. Stop twice to eject.
  2. Record Arm it, press play, then hit the woofers to record onto the loop.
  3. Tape label A text field. Name your mixtape.
  4. Bass boost A switch. The modern kind.
  5. VU meter The only part that just watches.
